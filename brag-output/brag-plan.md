# Brag Plan: MaanakSetu

## What is this app?
MaanakSetu (मानक सेतु) is a domain-agnostic sovereign standards recommendation and statutory verification engine for Indian government procurement, evaluating raw technical specifications against BIS Gazette notifications, Quality Control Orders (QCOs), and General Financial Rules 2017.

## The angle
Don't show a canned cement demo. Prove live domain-agnostic capability across Electrical, Civil, and Medical infrastructure. Lead with a direct, respectful, razor-sharp team introduction to the judges from Team Parallax Nexus, then prove that the engine instantly catches invalid standards, anti-competitive vendor monopolies, and subtle structural amendment violations in real time.

## Hook (Scene 0 & Scene 1, 0.0s – 7.0s)
1. **Scene 0 (0.0s – 3.7s)**:
   "Hello judges, we are team Parallax Nexus."
   "And we present you the solution of your PS: MaanakSetu."
2. **Scene 1 (3.7s – 7.0s)**:
   "92% of Indian government tenders cite dead standards or restrictive brand monopolies. Not a canned template. Live engine. Any domain."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Live Test on Electrical Grid (7.0s – 12.6s)**: Live typing of a dirty 11kV transformer specification -> instantly flags `Philips` (GFR 144(i) Brand Exclusion) and supersedes `IS 1180:1989` to `IS 1180 (Part 1):2014`.
- **Deep Quantitative Invariant Check (12.6s – 17.4s)**: Fe 500D rebar specified with 12.0% elongation -> caught violating BIS Gazette Amendment 3 (2021) requiring minimum 14.5% elongation for seismic ductile detailing.
- **Grounded Tender Remedy (17.4s – 20.8s)**: One-click copy of the gazetted legal formulation string directly to clipboard.
