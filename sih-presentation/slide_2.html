Problems & our Solutions

Superseded Standards & Legacy Citations

Statutory Defect

Tenders cite withdrawn codes (e.g. IS 269:1989 cement, IS 1180:1989 transformers), risking structural failure and statutory invalidation under GFR Rule 173.

MaanakSetu Remedy
• Automated Gazette reconciliation against active QCO standards.
• Normative reference DAG resolving cascading sub-clause dependencies.

No Centralized Real-Time Standards Registry

Statutory Defect

Gazetted amendments, QCOs, and validity dates are scattered across disparate PDFs, leaving officers without instant verification tools.

MaanakSetu Remedy
• MaanakSetu Knowledge Graph with real-time BIS Gazette synchronization.
• Sub-second structured lookup across 22,000+ national standards.

Restrictive Brand Exclusions (GFR 144(i))

Statutory Defect

Tenders mandate proprietary vendor makes ("UltraTech only", "Philips LED only"), violating GFR 144(i) and triggering CVC vigilance inquiries.

MaanakSetu Remedy
• NER + 1,200+ OEM Brand Dict to intercept proprietary bias.
• Auto-translation into neutral performance parameters.

Lack of Decision Support System for Corrigenda

Statutory Defect

Manual compliance audits take 5–7 days per tender; drafting legal corrigenda is ambiguous, error-prone, and causes litigation delays.

MaanakSetu Remedy
• Rule-based Decision Support System (DSS) generating legal corrigenda clauses.
• SHA-256 verified audit dossier for official vigilance defense.
